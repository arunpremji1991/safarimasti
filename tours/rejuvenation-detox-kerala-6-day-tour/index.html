About This Journey

This package pairs Safari Masti's Rejuvenation & Detoxification Healing Program — seven days of Ayurvedic treatment guided by Ayurvedic doctors — with a 6-day Kerala tour. After the programme you travel on to Cochin, Munnar, Thekkady and a houseboat night in Alleppey, ending with a drop-off at Cochin. Prefer a longer tour? See the 8-day option.

What Makes This Journey Special?

  • ✦7-day Rejuvenation & Detoxification Healing Program
  • ✦Two Ayurvedic therapies every day, with a daily doctor consultation
  • ✦Personalised diet, daily group yoga and meditation
  • ✦Then a Kerala tour: Cochin, Munnar, Thekkady and a night on an Alleppey houseboat

Your Journey

  1. Seven days of Ayurvedic rejuvenation and detoxification under the guidance of Ayurvedic doctors. After your first consultation, your doctor plans therapies, diet and routine around your constitution and goals. Every day includes two Ayurvedic therapies — such as Abhyangam (herbal oil massage), Shirodhara (medicated oil on the forehead) and Udvarthana (herbal powder massage), as prescribed — together with a personalised diet, group yoga, guided meditation and a doctor's consultation. Therapies and timings follow your doctor's plan.

  2. Your Kerala tour begins. Pick-up from Cochin Airport / Railway Station and drive to the hotel; check in and relax. Then Cochin sightseeing: Fort Cochin, the Jewish Synagogue, the Dutch Palace, Marine Drive and the Chinese fishing nets. Overnight at the hotel.

  3. After breakfast, check out and drive to Munnar. On the way, enjoy the Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls and a spice plantation visit. Check in and relax. In the evening you can enjoy a Kathakali and Kalaripayattu show (direct payment), an elephant ride (direct payment) and the Munnar market. Overnight in Munnar.

  4. After breakfast, Munnar sightseeing: the flower garden, photo point, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, Kundala Dam, Rajamalai (Eravikulam National Park) and Hydel Park. Overnight at the hotel.

  5. After breakfast, check out and drive to Thekkady; check in. You can enjoy Periyar boating (own payment) and an optional massage and jeep safari (direct payment). Overnight at the hotel.

  6. After breakfast, check out and drive to Alleppey; check into the houseboat. Enjoy Alleppey beach and local sightseeing. Full-board meals are provided on the houseboat. Overnight on the houseboat.

  7. After breakfast, check out from the houseboat and drop-off at Cochin Airport / Railway Station.

  • ✓Pick-up and drop-off for the Kerala tour
  • ✓3-star accommodation as per the tour itinerary

  • ✓English or Hindi speaking driver

Good to Know

The healing programme is a wellness programme guided by Ayurvedic doctors. It does not replace medical care. Please tell the doctors about any health condition or medication so your plan can be prepared safely.

About This Journey

A family-friendly circuit through the Himachal hills, starting and ending in Delhi. Spend two nights in Shimla with an excursion to Kufri and Jakhoo Temple, travel through the Kullu Valley to Manali for three nights of sightseeing, Hadimba Temple, Solang Valley and the Atal Tunnel, then finish with Nek Chand's Rock Garden and Sukhna Lake in Chandigarh before returning to Delhi. Breakfast and dinner are included, and you choose how you travel — a luxury Volvo coach with local transfers, a private sedan or a private SUV.

Your Journey

  1. Arrive in Delhi, with direct door-to-door pick-up from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) or New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS). Enjoy local sightseeing if time permits. Overnight at a Delhi hotel.

  2. Travel from Delhi to Shimla by car (by Volvo coach on Option A). Overnight stay in Shimla.

  3. A full day of local sightseeing in Shimla, including the full-day excursion to Kufri and Jakhoo Temple. Overnight stay in Shimla.

  4. Travel from Shimla to Manali through the breathtaking Kullu Valley. A welcome non-alcoholic drink is served on arrival at the mountain resort. Overnight stay in Manali.

  5. A full day in Manali with a local heritage walk to Hadimba Temple, Vashisht Springs and a Tibetan Monastery. Overnight stay in Manali.

  6. Head out from Manali to Solang Valley and drive through the Atal Tunnel — or, if you choose, to Rohtang Pass. The Rohtang Pass excursion needs an NGT permit and a local Manali Union 4x4 vehicle, which cost extra (see What's not included). Overnight stay in Manali.

  7. Check out from the hotel in Manali and travel to Chandigarh (by Volvo coach on Option A). Overnight stay in Chandigarh.

  8. Chandigarh urban sightseeing — Nek Chand's Rock Garden and Sukhna Lake. In the afternoon, travel to Delhi. Overnight stay at a Delhi hotel.

  9. Some shopping in Delhi if time permits, then drop-off at Delhi Airport or the railway station for your departure.

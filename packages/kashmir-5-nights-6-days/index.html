About This Journey

Land in Srinagar and settle onto a traditional Dal Lake houseboat, with an evening Shikara ride against the Zabarwan range. Day trips take you to Gulmarg's meadows and the Gondola Cable Car, and on to Pahalgam, the “Valley of Shepherds”, by way of the Awantipura ruins and saffron fields. Back in Srinagar you visit the Mughal Gardens of Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh and the Chashme Shahi spring, then spend a full day in Sonamarg, the “Meadow of Gold”. You travel by private A/c vehicle with an English-speaking driver cum guide, in 3-star or 4-star hotels as you choose.

Your Journey

  1. Our service begins with your arrival at Srinagar Airport, where our representative will meet you and transfer you to a traditional houseboat on Dal Lake. After relaxing for an hour, experience a peaceful evening Shikara ride exploring the interiors of Dal Lake against the backdrop of the stunning Zabarwan mountain range. Overnight stay at the Srinagar houseboat.

  2. After breakfast, drive to Gulmarg, a premier hill station celebrated for its lush slopes and vibrant wildflowers. In winter, it transforms into India's top skiing destination. You can ride the famous Gondola Cable Car to the Kongdori meadows and up to the Apharwat peak (14,000 ft) for sweeping Himalayan views. Drive back to Srinagar for an overnight stay.

  3. Enjoy an early breakfast, check out, and head to Pahalgam, the beautiful “Valley of Shepherds” and a favourite spot for Bollywood films. En route, stop by the historic Awantipura ruins and fragrant saffron fields. Once there, enjoy breathtaking views or opt for a local cab to visit the scenic Betaab Valley and Chandanwari (on direct payment). Overnight stay in Pahalgam.

  4. After breakfast, check out and drive back to Srinagar for a local sightseeing tour. Explore the iconic Mughal Gardens: Nishat Bagh (Garden of Pleasure) and Shalimar Bagh (Garden of Love), both featuring terraced flower beds and fountains overlooking Dal Lake. Conclude your tour at the Chashme Shahi (Royal Spring). Overnight stay at a hotel in Srinagar.

  5. After breakfast, embark on a full-day trip to Sonamarg, known as “The Meadow of Gold.” Surrounded by snow-capped peaks and dense pine forests, it serves as a spectacular gateway to Ladakh. Here, you can trek or take a pony ride to the stunning Thajiwas Glacier and admire the alpine meadows. Return to Srinagar for an overnight stay.

  6. After breakfast, check out from the hotel and enjoy a comfortable transfer to Srinagar Airport for your onward journey. Your magical Kashmir tour concludes here, leaving you with beautiful memories to cherish forever.

About This Journey

Start in Cochin and drive up to Munnar for Eravikulam National Park, a boat ride at Mattupetty or Kundala Dam, Echo Point and the Tata Tea Museum. Continue to Thekkady for a spice plantation tour and a boat safari in Periyar National Park, then spend a night on an Alleppey houseboat. Head south to Kanyakumari for the Vivekananda Rock Memorial, the Thiruvalluvar Statue, Bhagavathy Amman Temple and the sunset where three oceans meet, relax on the beach at Kovalam, and take a full-day Trivandrum tour before departing from Trivandrum or Cochin.

Your Journey

  1. On your arrival at Cochin Airport or Railway Station, our representative will greet you and assist with your transfer to Munnar, a breathtaking hill station 1,800 metres above sea level, famously known as a paradise for nature lovers. Along the scenic route, take a break to see the spectacular Cheyyappara Waterfalls. Arrive in Munnar, check into your chosen hotel or resort and enjoy a restful overnight stay.

  2. Explore the best tourist places in Munnar. Start your day with breakfast before a full day of sightseeing. The morning is dedicated to Eravikulam National Park, home to rare wildlife and panoramic mountain views. In the afternoon, enjoy a scenic boat ride at Mattupetty Dam or Kundala Dam, take photos at the popular Echo Point and discover local history at the Tata Tea Museum. Spend a relaxing evening at leisure. Overnight in Munnar.

  3. Travel from Munnar to the spice plantations of Thekkady. After breakfast, check out from your resort and head towards the lush forests of Thekkady. On arrival, check into your hotel. The afternoon features an educational spice plantation tour to see how local spices are grown. In the evening, experience a peaceful boat safari inside Periyar National Park, followed by free time for local shopping. Overnight in Thekkady.

  4. After breakfast, check out from your hotel in Thekkady and proceed to Alleppey — a captivating destination often called the “Venice of the East” for its network of canals. Renowned for its iconic houseboats, serene backwaters and pristine beaches, it is also famous for traditional coir crafts and fresh marine produce. Check in to the houseboat and start a peaceful backwater cruise on a traditional houseboat. Overnight on the Alleppey houseboat.

  5. After breakfast, check out from the houseboat and travel to Kanyakumari. After checking into your hotel, spend the afternoon exploring the iconic Vivekananda Rock Memorial, the towering Thiruvalluvar Statue and the historic Bhagavathy Amman Temple. End your day watching the spectacular sunset at Kanyakumari Beach, where three oceans meet (Triveni Sangam). Overnight in Kanyakumari.

  6. After breakfast, check out from your hotel and head towards Kovalam. On arrival, check into your beachside hotel or resort. Enjoy a completely free afternoon to unwind, stroll along the shore and take in the beautiful beach vibes. Spend the night resting comfortably in Kovalam.

  7. After breakfast, leave for a full day of sightseeing in Trivandrum. Begin with a visit to the magnificent Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple to explore its grand heritage. Afterwards, head to the city's museum grounds to visit the Napier Museum, the Sree Chitra Art Gallery and the historic Thiruvananthapuram Zoo. Return to Kovalam in the evening for your overnight stay.

  8. After breakfast, check out of the hotel. We will drop you at the airport or railway station in either Trivandrum or Cochin for your trip home. Your tour ends with beautiful memories.

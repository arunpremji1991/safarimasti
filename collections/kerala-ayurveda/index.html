The Therapies

What your stay is made of

Each programme draws on classical Ayurvedic therapies. Here is what the treatments named in our programmes actually are.

Essential Body Refresh

Abhyangam — Full-Body Oil Massage

A rhythmic massage with warm herbal oils, traditionally given by two therapists working together. In the Ayurvedic tradition it is used to ease muscle tension, settle the nerves and prepare the body for the therapies that follow.

Essential Body Refresh

Herbal Steam Bath

Warm herbal steam taken in a traditional wooden chamber with the head kept outside. It is customarily paired with oil massage to leave the body feeling light, warm and deeply relaxed.

Deep Stress Relief

Shirodhara — Warm Oil Pouring on the Forehead

A thin, steady stream of warm herbal oil poured slowly over the forehead while you lie at rest. It is one of Ayurveda's most recognised therapies for calming an overactive mind.

Deep Stress Relief

Herbal Pouch Massage

Warm cloth pouches filled with herbs and medicinal powders, heated over steam and pressed gently over the body in a steady rhythm to release tension.

Total Body Detox & Reset

Panchakarma — Classical Cleansing Therapies

Panchakarma, meaning “five actions”, is Ayurveda's classical system of internal and external cleansing. In your programme it is followed under the guidance of our Ayurvedic doctors, together with tissue-strengthening massages and a personalised diet.

Part of every stay

Yoga & Guided Meditation

Gentle morning yoga to wake the body, and an evening guided meditation to close the day — an everyday rhythm that carries the calm of the therapies into the rest of your time.

Good to Know

Questions, answered

How long should I stay?+

It depends on what you want from the stay. Essential Body Refresh is 3 to 5 days for a short, effective break; Deep Stress Relief is 7 days; Total Body Detox & Reset is 14 days or more for deeper, longer-term goals.

Who prescribes and performs the treatments?+

Treatments are prescribed by certified traditional Ayurvedic doctors and performed by experienced therapists, with daily consultations throughout your stay.

What about food?+

Three tailored vegetarian meals a day are part of every stay, designed for your body type. Detox programmes follow a stricter personalised diet.

Do I need yoga experience?+

The daily yoga is gentle, and the evening meditation is guided, so both are suited to those new to them.

Can I combine it with sightseeing in Kerala or India?+

Yes. Safari Masti designs custom journeys across India, so we can plan Kerala sightseeing or another destination before or after your Ayurveda stay.

How do I get prices and dates?+

Every programme is shaped around your dates and goals, so we prepare a personal proposal rather than a fixed price list. Message us on WhatsApp or use Build Your Journey and we will get back to you.

Is Ayurveda a substitute for medical care?+

No. These are wellness programmes. Please tell our doctors about any health condition or medication so your programme can be planned safely, and keep following your own doctor's advice.

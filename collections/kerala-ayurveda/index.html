The Programme

What your healing programme is made of

The seven days draw on classical Ayurvedic therapies and a daily rhythm of yoga, meditation and carefully planned meals. Here is what the treatments are.

Part of the daily therapies

Abhyangam — Herbal Oil Massage

A rhythmic massage with warm herbal oils, given by trained therapists. In the Ayurvedic tradition it is used to ease muscle tension, settle the nerves and prepare the body for the therapies that follow.

Part of the daily therapies

Shirodhara — Warm Oil on the Forehead

A thin, steady stream of warm medicated oil poured slowly over the forehead while you lie at rest. It is one of Ayurveda's most recognised therapies for calming an overactive mind.

Part of the daily therapies

Udvarthana — Herbal Powder Massage

A massage with dry herbal powders, applied in firm, upward strokes. Traditionally it is used to stimulate circulation and leave the body feeling light and refreshed.

Every day

Group Yoga & Guided Meditation

Yoga and breathing practice in the morning, following your doctor's advice, and a guided meditation in the evening to settle the mind before rest.

Every day

A Personalised Diet

Three meals a day planned by your doctor around your constitution and digestion — light, nourishing food, freshly prepared and easy to digest.

Good to Know

Questions, answered

How long is the package?+

Every package starts with a 7-day Rejuvenation & Detoxification Healing Program. After it you continue on a Kerala tour of your choice: 6 days (13 days in total) or 8 days (15 days in total).

What does the healing programme include?+

A daily consultation with your Ayurvedic doctor, two Ayurvedic therapies every day, a personalised diet, group yoga and guided meditation. Therapies and timings follow your doctor's plan.

Who plans and performs the treatments?+

Ayurvedic doctors plan your programme around your constitution and goals and review it with you daily; trained therapists carry out the therapies.

Can I choose my tour?+

Yes. After the 7 days of treatment you choose either the 6-day Kerala tour, or the 8-day tour that adds Kovalam beach and Trivandrum.

What is included in the tour?+

Pick-up and drop-off, 3-star accommodation as per the itinerary, a houseboat stay at Alleppey with meals on board, an English or Hindi speaking driver and AC private transportation. Entry tickets, optional activities, airfare and GST are not included — each package page lists everything.

How do I get prices and dates?+

Every package is shaped around your dates and goals, so we prepare a personal proposal rather than a fixed price list. Message us on WhatsApp or use Build Your Journey and we will get back to you.

Is Ayurveda a substitute for medical care?+

No. This is a wellness programme. Please tell our doctors about any health condition or medication so your programme can be planned safely, and keep following your own doctor's advice.
